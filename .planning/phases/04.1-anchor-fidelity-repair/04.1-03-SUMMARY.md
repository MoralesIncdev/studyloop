## The pearl rule's three outcomes (exact)

1. **In range** (`t <= ` the last caption segment's `end`) → left untouched, byte-identical, whether or not it links to a unit.
2. **Out of range + linked (`unitId`) to a unit with at least one corrected anchor** → `t` moved to that unit's earliest anchor time (the only position the pass can defend — a pearl has no quote of its own to locate by).
3. **Out of range + no usable link** (`unitId` absent, or naming a unit with zero anchors, or naming a unit that doesn't exist) → left untouched, recorded unresolved. A timestamp is never invented, never clamped to the transcript end.

## Telemetry log format (exact)

Silent when a run corrects nothing (`corrections.length === 0 && pearlCorrections.length === 0`). Otherwise:

```
[analysis] anchor-correction: units=<N> safetyTier=<N> pearls=<N> unresolved=<N>
[analysis] anchor-correction detail: unit=<id> type=<UnitType> before=<t> after=<t>
```

One detail line per unit correction (not per pearl correction — the plan's own spec names only "the unit id, its type, and the before and after values" for detail lines). `unresolved` counts unresolved unit-anchor QUOTES specifically; pearls carry no quote so `unresolvedPearls` isn't folded into that count.
