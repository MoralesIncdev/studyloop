<objective>
Prove the whole Phase 04.1 architecture on one thin end-to-end path before building any of it out: a rolling-caption transcript goes in, and a verifier-clean analysis comes out — with the caption dedup (D-01 item 1), the post-merge anchor-correction pass (D-01 item 2), and the committed anchor verifier all wired together in a single deterministic test.

Purpose: The one architectural risk that could sink this phase is the corrector and the verifier disagreeing — a corrector that snaps `t` using its own matching logic could "fix" anchors the verifier still rejects, and the phase would burn a live LLM re-run (D-02) to discover it. Wiring one path through every layer first, on this agent's best early-context tokens, catches that after one commit instead of after five. This is a tracer, not a prototype: everything written here is production code that survives into the final system; the expansion plans widen it, they do not replace it.

Output: `server/src/lib/rollingCaptions.ts` (rolling-cue dedup), `server/src/lib/anchorCorrect.ts` (post-merge anchor correction), a shared quote-occurrence finder exported from `server/src/lib/anchorVerify.ts`, both wired into `captionParse.ts` and `runAnalysisJobV3`, and `server/test/anchorFidelity.test.ts` carrying the end-to-end proof.
</objective>
