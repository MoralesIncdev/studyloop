  `.planning/codebase/TESTING.md` records component tests as a known accepted gap. Rather
  than stand up jsdom and a testing-library dependency for one chip, the warning's logic
  lives in a pure, node-testable helper and the render itself is covered by typecheck, build,
  and the Task 2 human check. Establishing a component-test pattern is a separate decision.

  an SVG icon-system choice. Left untouched; the NEW warning is glyph-free and asserted so.

  strict trimmed-length rule adopted in plan 01-02. Recorded as a Phase 4 proof-run watch item.
